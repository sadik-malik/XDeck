## Horizontal Spacing

**Impact: LOW (consistent spacing within a line)**

- One space after commas, around `a + b`, after `if (`/`while (` — not between `foo` and `()` .
- No trailing whitespace. Consistent indent (2 or 4 spaces, or tabs) — never mix.
